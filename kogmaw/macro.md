# Kog'Maw — Macro

Lane states and map play for Kog'Maw bot. Sources: this folder's `playbook.md` (the source guide is mostly build), Mobalytics and Dignitas bot-lane guides, and general wave guides (Guild Order). No Kog'Maw games logged yet. Matchup notes stay in `matchups.md`.

## Lane states

1. **Level 2 takes 9 minions in a duo lane:** the first wave of 6 plus 3 melee of the second. If you lose the race, back off behind your wave or to your tower until it pushes to you.
2. **Farm, don't fight.** Pre-Guinsoo's your range is short; play at max range, wiggle before last hits, and skip extended trades.
3. **Don't stand too close to the wave** in poke lanes; you're the target and you have no escape.
4. **Let the wave come to you when you're weak.** A wave pushing to your tower is safe farm for an immobile carry. Shove only with vision and a reason (recall, drake).
5. **Crash, then recall.** Crash the wave so the back is safe and the next wave comes to you. Bot laners usually back when they can buy a major item.
6. **Crash before drake.** Pushing bot before drake spawns gives your jungler priority.

## After laning

- After the first bot tower falls, move to mid: the shortest walk to every fight.
- Take a side wave only when their jungler and mid are seen on the far side; leave when that changes. Count your solo side-lane deaths; if any, stop taking side waves.
- Group. Never split push: no mobility, no escape. Stand behind your tank and support every fight.
- Ahead: push lanes and look for picks with your team nearby. Behind or even: play like early, wait for them to burn abilities, then W them down.
- A 20-minute Kog'Maw with one item is dangerous, but only if he's alive. Scaling is the win condition.

## Drills

- Count deaths in lane where you had no vision or no support nearby.
- Crash before every recall, for a week.
- In fights, wait for the enemy's first engage before stepping into range.
