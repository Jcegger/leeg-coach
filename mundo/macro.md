## Lane states

1. **Concede the level-2 race.** Solo lane hits level 2 at 7 minions: the 6-minion first wave plus one melee of the second. Whoever pushes wave one gets there first, and that's rarely you. Against early spikers, step back before their 7th minion dies; trade once you're level 2 too.
2. **Shove by default.** Freezing into Mundo leaves the enemy outside their minions and open to cleavers, so they rarely manage it. Farm with autos where you can: a missed rank-1 cleaver costs 50 HP.
3. **Spend every crash.** After shoving, back, ward or take plates. Standing at their tower with no vision is how the shove turns into a gank.
4. **Thin their slow push.** Cleaver it, or E a minion into it, so a big wave never crashes into your tower. That's the dive setup, and losing waves to your tower hurts even if the dive trades 1-for-1.
5. **Back on a crash, never on a pushing wave.** Recalling while your wave pushes toward you hands them a free shove or a freeze.
6. **Plates on their back.** After 5:00, when they recall and your wave is in, auto plus E their tower for two plates.
7. **Behind? Stay in XP range.** Give up CS before you give up a death. One more early death snowballs.
8. **Respect 2:45-3:15.** You're weakest when early-pressure junglers look for their first gank. Don't be shoved past river with their jungler unseen.
9. **First cannon wave: trade only with the bigger wave.** Mundo can't win a trade inside their wave.
10. **Plan the quest TP.** Ghost + Ignite, and the top quest hands you Unleashed Teleport around 13-14 min. Crash, then TP to the next drake or a bot dive.
11. **Never ult to heal in lane.** Rank-1 R barely heals and the cooldown is long. If you're that low, back.

## After laning (Warmog's + Heartsteel, or level 11)

- The quest lifts your level cap to 20: more R and stats for the split push.
- Split, then TP: with quest TP up, crash a side wave and TP to the fight or objective.
- Split push for tower gold, wave XP and camps. Your tower damage is the highest of any tank, and E shreds monsters.
- Slow push a wave about a minute before drake or herald, so the enemy chooses between the objective and their tower.
- You're an Illaoi/Sion split pusher: you win a split by killing whoever answers, not by running away. If you can't beat the responder, group.
- "If you don't know what to do, just group." Splitting is often the wrong call; grouping rarely is.
