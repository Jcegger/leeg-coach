# General — Macro

Rules for every champion and role. Sources: Dignitas, LoL Sensei, mid.gg, Shelbion, Boosteria, Guild Order, MetaBot, Level Legendary, and 2026 timer guides (gamerstogether, neuralboost). Champion-specific notes live on each champion's page.

## Timers (2026)

- Drake 5:00, then 5:00 after each kill.
- Voidgrubs 8:00: one group of 3, no respawn. They leave around 14:45.
- Herald 15:00. Sources disagree on when it leaves; check in game.
- Baron 20:00. Atakhan was removed in 26.1.
- leeg's objectives line shows these live.

## Trading around the wave

- The bigger wave wins trades. Never fight inside their bigger wave: every minion nearby switches to you.
- Pull them out of their wave first, or thin their casters before you commit.
- Cannon waves are the trading windows: both players walk up for the cannon. The first cannon comes on wave 3.
- Missing one cannon is cheaper than dying.

## Tracking the jungler

1. **Leash at 1:25.** Watch which side their bot lane walks toward, or shows up late from: their jungler started on that side.
2. **The 2:45-3:15 window.** Early-pressure junglers (Lee Sin, Elise, Jarvan IV) are level 3 by 2:45 and look for a gank before 3:15. Don't be shoved with no vision then.
3. **A sighting is a clock.** Jungler seen bot means a pushed top lane has about 8-10 seconds of safety. Cleared camps show where they've been.

## Objectives

- Start the slow push 2-3 waves (about a minute) before the spawn, so the crash lands as it spawns.
- Shoved under your own tower with no priority? Don't contest; let it go.
- Crash before you rotate, or the trip costs you a wave and plates.

## Teleport (top lane role quest)

- **Top always gets TP.** The quest (1,200 points) hands you Unleashed Teleport on a 390 s cooldown, plus a level 20 cap and +600 XP. Points: 1.5/s anywhere outside your base, 2 per minion, 40 per plate, 50 per turret, 15 per takedown, 30 per epic monster. Recalling pauses the trickle for 12 s. (SenseOS, patch 26.21.)
- **It lands around 13-14 min** (my estimate from those rates): sooner with plates and CS, later with backs and deaths. Dying early costs you TP and level 20 too.
- **One TP per drake cycle.** At 390 s, spend it on objectives or bot dives, not on walking back to lane.
- **Crash first.** TP to a fight only when your wave won't punish it.
- Taking Teleport as a spell instead trades the free one for a 35% max-HP shield on arrival; the champion pages assume you don't.

## Side lanes

- Ward the river or jungle entrance before pushing past the middle of a side lane, not after you get collapsed on.
- Fight only when the numbers change (two of them show up for you).
- Don't keep farming a side lane while the game is decided without you.

## Playing from behind

- Give up waves rather than die. Skip skirmishes you can't win.
- Tanks recover cheaply: tank items cost less, and you can buy straight into your laner's damage type.
- Catch up with Herald, tower farm and safe waves.
