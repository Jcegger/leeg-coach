# Olaf — Build

Source: Federals1's "[26.21] Challenger Olaf Top Guide" (Mobafire, updated 2026-11-02). Top lane.

## Summoner spells

- **Standard:** Flash + Ghost
- **Optional:** Ghost + Teleport
- **Matchup-specific:** Exhaust + Ghost (lanes you can't run down otherwise)

## Runes

### Standard (Conqueror)
- **Precision (primary):** Conqueror · Triumph · Legend: Bloodline · Last Stand
- **Inspiration (secondary):** Magical Footwear · Approach Velocity
- **Shards:** +9 Adaptive · +2.5% Move Speed · +10–180 HP

Bloodline is the default since 14.21, always with Ravenous Hydra (more lifesteal = bigger Hydra heal). Alacrity instead when you don't build Ravenous Hydra.

### Lethal Tempo (crit build only)
- **Precision (primary):** Lethal Tempo · Triumph · Legend: Alacrity · Last Stand
- **Inspiration (secondary):** Magical Footwear · Approach Velocity
- **Shards:** +9 Adaptive · +2.5% Move Speed · +10–180 HP

**Notes:** Conqueror 99.9% of the time. Magical Footwear plus the move-speed shard lets you outrun tier-2 boots with no gold spent. Approach Velocity pairs with the Q slow. Resolve secondary (Second Wind / Bone Plating / Revitalize) is the option into poke or combo lanes (Sett, Riven, Renekton).

## Starting items

Doran's Blade · Health Potion (Doran's Shield into poke lanes).

Try to kill your laner before the 4th wave, shove, and recall. First blood: Vampiric Scepter first, it wins almost every melee lane. Otherwise a cheater recall: Long Sword / Cloth Armor / Ruby Crystal / Boots. Matchup-specific first buys: Bramble Vest, Hexdrinker, Phage, Warden's Mail, Executioner's Calling.

## Build paths

### Current build (go-to)
1. Ravenous Hydra
2. Plated Steelcaps
3. Experimental Hexplate
4. Maw of Malmortius
5. Death's Dance
6. Endless Hunger

The guide's standard build. Hydra + Hexplate + Steelcaps is the core; Hydra, Hexplate, Death's Dance and Maw is the real "full build", the sooner the better. Endless Hunger 4th-5th gives about 45 ability haste from Olaf's bonus AD.

2. Ravenous Hydra
3. Experimental Hexplate
4. Spirit Visage
5. Jak'Sho, The Protean

Guide's "vs heavy AP". Maw's shield goes past 800 with your R active.

3. Frozen Heart

Guide's "vs heavy AD". Warden's Mail can sit unfinished if you don't need the attack-speed slow.

More health, less burst; the Stridebreaker slow catches ranged and kiting champions. Black Cleaver fits here vs heavy-armor tanks (Malphite, Ornn, Rammus).

1. Ravenous Hydra
2. Fiendhunter Bolts
3. Plated Steelcaps

Guide's crit set; Infinity Edge, Yun Tal Wildarrows and Phantom Dancer are the other crit options.

## Situational item swaps

Grounded in the go-to path.

- Stridebreaker over Ravenous Hydra — ranged or high-mobility enemies you can't catch; you lose lifesteal but keep up with them
- Ionian Boots of Lucidity over Plated Steelcaps — little physical damage on their team; haste on ult and Ghost
- Mercury's Treads over Plated Steelcaps — 3+ AP enemies with heavy hard CC (your R already ignores CC, so only when it's swarming)
- Black Cleaver over Endless Hunger — 2+ armor-stacking tanks (Malphite, Ornn, Rammus)
- Chempunk Chainsword over Endless Hunger — healers; the offensive anti-heal
- Thornmail over Death's Dance — healers or attack-speed carries; finish the Bramble Vest you bought in lane
- Randuin's Omen over Death's Dance — 2+ crit carries (ADC + Yasuo/Yone), or one SNOWBALLING crit carry
- Sterak's Gage over Death's Dance — one big burst you have to live through (Darius or Garen ult)
- Guardian Angel over Endless Hunger — late game, when one teamfight decides it

## Do NOT buy

- Wit's End — tenacity is wasted while R makes you CC-immune, and the damage is weak
- Blade of the Ruined King — your Q already shreds tanks; regular build does as much to Cho'Gath / Mundo
- Tier-3 boots — top laners can't get them since 26.1

## Skill order

**Max order:** E > Q > W (the guide's order since 26.1). R at 6/11/16. Take W at level 2 in lanes you can all-in early.
