# Olaf — Macro

Lane states and map play for Olaf top. Sources: Federals1's guide (cached Nov 10, `meta.json`) and general wave guides (Guild Order, MetaBot, Mobalytics). No Olaf games logged yet, so no numbers of your own. Matchup-specific wave notes stay in `matchups.md`.

## Lane states

1. **Play for the level-2 race.** Solo lane hits level 2 at 7 minions: the first wave of 6 plus one melee of the second. Levels 1-3 are Olaf's strongest; take W at 2 in lanes you can all-in and threaten anyone who walks up for last hits.
2. **Your Q pushes the wave for you.** That leaves you shoved and gankable. Before 6, back off a gank; with R up you can often kill both.
3. **Kill before the 4th wave, then shove and recall.** If the kill isn't there, crash the wave and take a cheater recall: back as it hits their tower, return as it bounces to you.
4. **First blood means Vampiric Scepter.** It gives lane control in almost every melee matchup.
5. **Proxy once you have Tiamat.** Two Q hits clear a wave. Farm between their towers (as deep as their inhib turret) so they must answer or lose waves, then back for free: you'll be back before the wave arrives. They can't recall without losing a whole wave.
6. **Check the jungler before each shove.** Your Q pushes for you, and the kill-before-wave-4 window overlaps their 2:45-3:15 gank window.
7. **Back on a crash, never on a pushing wave.** Recalling with your wave pushing toward you hands them a free shove or a freeze.

## After laning (Ravenous Hydra + Experimental Hexplate)

- This is your peak. Fight on 2-item spikes and push the lead into objectives; aim to end by 25 minutes.
- Split the side lane opposite the main objective; 1v1 or 2v1 whoever answers if you're ahead. Dive and take the tower when one person comes.
- If your laner counters you and also wins side lane, group and hunt immobile targets instead.
- Flash + Ghost, so the quest's Unleashed TP (around 13-14 min) is free. With a lead, crash and TP into drake fights.
- Slow push a wave about a minute before drake or herald, so the enemy chooses between the objective and their tower.

## Drills

- Count the 7th minion every game: know who hits level 2 first before it happens.
- Crash before every recall, for a week.
- Note the minute your first item lands; Olaf's value drops every minute it's late.
