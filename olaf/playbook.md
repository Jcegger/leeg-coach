# Olaf — Playbook

From Federals1's Challenger Olaf guide (Mobafire, 2026-11-02), condensed.

## Win conditions

- **Win lane early.** Levels 1-3 are strong: chain Q hits and threaten to kill anyone who walks up for last hits. Aim to kill your laner before the 4th wave, shove, and recall.
- **Mid game is the peak.** Ravenous Hydra + Experimental Hexplate (then Death's Dance + Maw) is when Olaf takes over; get there fast.
- **End by 25 minutes.** Olaf falls off hard late unless the comp is built for him (or you have Taric). Push the lead into objectives instead of farming for later.
- **R is the go button.** CC immunity beats most peel; immobile targets (Cho'Gath, Sion, Ornn, Ashe) die if you land Q and ult. Dashes and stalls beat it, so chain axes and don't run in on a high-mobility carry without Ghost.

## Side selection

Split push the side lane opposite the main objective and force someone to answer; 1v1 or 2v1 the responders if you're ahead. If your laner is a direct counter who also wins side lane, group instead and look for the immobile targets in teamfights. Proxy farming between their turrets works once you have Tiamat: two Q's clear the wave, and they must answer or lose waves.

## Early game

Your passive gets stronger as your health drops, so keep fighting when it looks bad. W at level 2 in lanes you can all-in. You auto-push with Q, so watch for ganks; with R up you often kill both players, but before 6 back off. After first blood, Vampiric Scepter first gives lane control in almost every melee matchup. Level 6 at your tower: land Q, ult (and Ghost) and they're dead. Early pressure means priority on your side's Voidgrubs.

## Mid game

Look for 2-item spikes and fight on them. Into champions that want to CC and kite you (Ashe), pop R and run them down; they need enough damage to burst you, not peel. Split push the far side and take turrets when one person answers.

## Late game

Damage ramps up and you get burst if you run in headfirst. Wait for a pick, then snowball the fight off that first kill. Champions you beat early can out-duel you now; don't take side-lane 1v1s against counterpicks.

## Teamfighting

Start with Q to shred armor, then E, auto, W to reset (W also takes 2s off E), Hydra active. E is true damage that costs health: at low health, auto first and use E as an execute. E extends R like an auto, so save it for a target out of auto range. E on the target also skips Bramble Vest's heal cut. Save R for the objective fight unless the solo kill is on a priority carry.
