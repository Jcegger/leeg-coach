# Shen — Macro

Lane states and map play for Shen top. Sources: the My Immortal guide (`meta.json`; it covers builds, not waves), this folder's `playbook.md`, Skill-Capped and Tacter Shen guides, and general wave guides (Guild Order, MetaBot). One Shen game logged, too few for numbers. Matchup-specific wave notes stay in `matchups.md`.

## Lane states

1. **Don't lose lane; you don't need to win it.** 0/0 with full CS beats trading kills. Short trades only (E, Q-empowered autos, W for their key ability).
2. **Level 2 at 7 minions.** Solo lane: the first wave of 6 plus one melee of the second. Respect it against early all-in laners.
3. **Shen's wave clear is weak.** Plan pushes a wave ahead: start a slow push early rather than trying to shove one wave late.
4. **Shove before you ult.** A wave left pushing toward your tower while you R loses plates or the tower. Crash first, then R when the timing allows.
5. **Back on a crash, never on a pushing wave.** Recalling while your wave pushes toward you hands them a free shove or a freeze. First back at about 1050 gold.

## After laning

- Split the lane furthest from the active objective, so the enemy chooses between you and the objective.
- R plus the quest's Unleashed TP (around 13-14 min) gives you two globals. Split harder once both are up: R the fight, TP the objective.
- Split only with R or TP up. With both on cooldown, stay with your team until they're back.
- First R matters: use it when an ally is being dove and the 3s channel will land, not on a fight that's already decided. After a won fight near an objective, stay and take it.
- Slow push a wave about a minute before drake or herald so the crash pressures their tower while you R in.

## Drills

- Before every R, check your wave: crashing or at least not pushing into your tower.
- Track how many of your ults gave a kill or saved an ally.
- Crash before every recall, for a week.
