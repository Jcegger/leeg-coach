# Sivir — Macro

Lane states and map play for Sivir bot. Sources: this folder's `playbook.md` (the source guide is build-only), Mobalytics and Dignitas bot-lane guides, the Giga Wave Clear Sivir guide, and general wave guides (Guild Order). Two Sivir games logged, too few for numbers. Matchup notes stay in `matchups.md`.

## Lane states

1. **Level 2 takes 9 minions in a duo lane:** the first wave of 6 plus 3 melee of the second. Shove wave one with your support to get there first and step up. If you lose the race, back off behind your wave or to your tower until it pushes to you.
2. **You're not a lane bully.** Farm safely and trade only with a clear edge. Get to mid game even; you don't need to win lane.
3. **W shoves harder than almost anyone.** Use it on purpose: to set up your support's roam, a dive, or a recall. Every shove needs a plan.
4. **Ward before you shove.** Sivir has no escape; a shoved Sivir without tribush vision is the easiest gank on the map. Control ward first.
5. **Crash, then recall.** Crashing the wave is what makes a back safe. Bot laners usually back when they can buy a major item; crash first so the next wave comes to you.
6. **Crash before drake.** Pushing bot into their tower before drake spawns gives your jungler priority on the objective.

## After laning (Yun Tal online)

- After the first bot tower falls, move to mid: the shortest walk to every fight.
- Take a side wave only when their jungler and mid are seen on the far side; leave when that changes. Count your solo side-lane deaths; if any, stop taking side waves.
- Fight or take an objective right after the spike.
- Push side waves into towers, then rotate to grouped objectives. Your clear lets you be the lane pressuring sides while the team takes drake or baron.
- Push side lanes before major objectives (baron, elder), then group.
- Default is to group. Side lane only when your team is busy with another objective and R is up for the rotation back. Never 1v1.

## Drills

- Call the level-2 race in the first 90 seconds: who pushed wave one?
- Control ward before every shove, for a week.
- Count deaths with no vision near you; aim for zero.
